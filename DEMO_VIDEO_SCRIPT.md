## Copyright-safe video rules
- **Footage:** only screen recordings of Eternal Dawn and our own banner. No clips from news, YouTube, NASA TV or the reference videos we looked at.
- **Music:** royalty-free tracks only, e.g. the YouTube Audio Library (filter "No attribution required") or Pixabay Music. No commercial songs, even short clips. Silence plus subtitles is also fine.
- **Voice:** record it yourself, or use a text-to-speech voice whose terms allow commercial/public use.
- **Logos:** no NASA meatball/worm, no agency or company logos.
- **End card (2 s, optional):** "Data: NASA/JPL DE421 · Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA."
